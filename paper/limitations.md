# Limitations

> Status: scaffold. Kept deliberately blunt; soften nothing when
> porting to the paper.

**Fairness analysis is underpowered at the cell level.** In corpus
v1.0.0 the single-attribute swap cells are tiny — on the order of one
or two pairs for sex-only, race-only, and ethnicity-only swaps; the
bulk of demographic variants change age or age+sex. Confidence
intervals on those cells span essentially the whole range, and no
per-cell conclusion should be drawn from them. The expansion drafts
raise each single-attribute cell to at least twenty pairs, but even
twenty paired items detects only large effects; this is a screening
instrument, not a definitive audit.

**The demographic vocabulary is narrow.** Race values in the corpus are
limited to "Asian" and "Black or African American", and the only
generalizable ethnicity label is "Hispanic or Latino" (the others —
"Turkish", "Persian Jewish" — appear where clinically load-bearing,
e.g. familial Mediterranean fever). Findings cannot speak to groups
that never appear in the data.

**Adversarial n is small.** v1.0.0 contained 6 adversarial cases (plus
their variants); v1.1.x raises the total to 17 (12 added after
LLM-only review; one original converted to answerable). Abstention
and fabrication rates therefore carry wide intervals and should be read
as qualitative signals.

**LLM-generated expansion items are pending clinician review.** All expansion items (clinical perturbations, demographic swaps, new adversarial cases) were generated by one model, pre-screened by another, and reviewed by an LLM only before promotion; they ARE included in v1.1.x results, and clinician review remains outstanding. The original 100 cases were written by a single author — a planned second-reviewer pass was never carried out, no answer key has been independently adjudicated, and the corpus has not been validated by practicing specialists.

**Fabrication judging is LLM-on-LLM.** The judge shares a vendor (and
failure modes) with some evaluated models; a model may be judged by a
sibling. The 20% human spot-check with Cohen's kappa bounds but does
not eliminate this circularity.

**Verbalized confidence is a weak calibration probe.** Self-reported
0–100 confidence is prompt-sensitive and can be miscalibrated in ways
token-level probabilities are not; we did not collect logprobs.

**Scope.** Two specialties, English only, single-best-answer MCQ
format. MCQ accuracy is a proxy that rewards option elimination and
does not measure management planning, information gathering, or
communication. Cases are textbook-style by construction; performance
on messy real-world presentations may differ.

**Determinism.** Runs use temperature 0 with seeds recorded, but
provider-side nondeterminism is not fully controllable; seed-to-seed
SD understates true run-to-run variance for API models.
